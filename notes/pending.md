# Pending — not yet in the piece

## A twenty-fifth line

    buys me ice cream every month for our anniversary

In second person, to match the rest:

    You buy me ice cream every month for our anniversary

It is one of the strongest lines in the whole note: it is literally one per
month, so twenty-four months is twenty-four ice creams. The form is already
about counting to twenty-four, and this is the only line that counts with it.

**The problem is the count.** The piece is twenty-four things. Adding this
makes twenty-five, and every direction is built on twenty-four — the score
bars, the two rings of twelve, the running foot that reads "| 24".

**Least-loss way to make room.** Two lines both say empathetic:

    11  You are very empathetic, my twin for real
    22  You're empathetic and feel your emotions

Merged, keeping every phrase from both:

    You are very empathetic, my twin for real. You feel your emotions

That frees exactly one slot and loses no words. Nothing else in the note is
this close to being a duplicate.

Not applied. This is the author's call.
