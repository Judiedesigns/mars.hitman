# The edit pass — before and after

One rule, applied throughout: **the note slips between talking *to* him and
*about* him.** "I like how **you** take on tasks" sits next to "I love how
**he** is passionate". Since this is a letter addressed to him, second person
is the spine and the third-person lines move to "you".

Everything else is left alone. "maddd" stays. "my twin for real" stays.
"Dams sends me money" stays exactly as written — it is the funniest and most
specific line in the note and any polish would kill it.

## Two you need to decide, not me

**08 and 09** were "Is very helpful" and "You are so incredibly talented and
helpful" — helpful twice. I split them so 08 carries helpful and 09 carries
talented. If the repetition was the point, say so and I'll put it back.

**11 and 22** were both "empathetic". I left empathetic on 11 and turned 22
toward feeling his own emotions, which is what that line seems to be reaching
for. Same offer.
