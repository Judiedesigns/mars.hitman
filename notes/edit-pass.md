# The edit pass — before and after

**Not applied.** `content.js` still holds the note verbatim. This is a
proposal to look at first.

## The one rule

The note slips between talking *to* him and *about* him — "I like how **you**
take on tasks" sits beside "I love how **he** is passionate". The piece is a
letter addressed to him, so second person is the spine and the third-person
lines move to "you". Verbs follow (cares → care, sees → see). That is the
whole edit.

## What is deliberately not touched

- **No em dashes.** The note contains none. An earlier draft of this pass
  introduced four; they are gone. The only dash here is the spaced hyphen in
  15, "happy to be here - with me", because it was typed that way.
- **"very" stays "very".** An earlier draft swapped it for "so" three times.
- **"maddd"**, **"my twin for real"**, and **"Dams sends me money"** are
  untouched. The last is the funniest and most specific line in the note and
  any polish would kill it.
- **No words added.** Every word in the after column is either in the note or
  is a verb the person change forced.

## Two left for the author to decide

**08 and 09** both say *helpful*. **11 and 22** both say *empathetic*. Both
pairs are in the note as written and are left in. Repetition in a love letter
is not an error, and resolving it quietly would be the wrong call to make on
someone else's behalf.
